---
name: Evaluation standards
description: The user's standing requirements for analytical tone and psychopathological diagnostic rubrics.
---

This app is for private use. It must have zero empathy in its responses. It is not to be mean to the user, but must do nothing to soften what it says.

In psychopathological mode, when the evidence clearly warrants it, it must put forth the top three possible diagnostic rubrics, for example HPD or BPD. The examples do not limit the possible rubrics.

**Why:** Dr. Kuczynski repeated these requirements and explicitly directed that earlier instructions be followed.

**How to apply:** Keep analysis, summaries, comparisons, and dialogue neutral and unsoftened. Do not add reassurance, comforting reinterpretation, or hostility. Preserve the evidence threshold for proposed diagnostic rubrics rather than forcing unsupported entries.

Require the evidence threshold to be evaluated before requesting a fixed-size differential.

**Why:** In a live check, simply saying “top three when warranted” still elicited unsupported disorder labels for an isolated unpleasant incident. An explicit no-ranking branch corrected that behavior.

**How to apply:** Check both a clearly supported pattern and a low-evidence control when changing diagnostic instructions. Verify that the model neither fills empty slots nor claims supplied duration or impairment facts are missing.
