---
name: Report length
description: The user's definitions of Micro, Normal, and Macro output length.
---

Micro means two paragraphs, or the equivalent in compact points. Normal means about five paragraphs. Macro means ten paragraphs.

**Why:** The user corrected excessively long Micro analyses: these are lengths for the whole report, not for each evaluation question.

**How to apply:** Count the overview, analytical findings, and any warranted diagnostic ranking within the same report budget. Do not turn the internal evaluation checklist into a long sequence of separately printed answers.
